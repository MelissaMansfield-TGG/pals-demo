P.A.L.S. Pet Awareness for Living Safely
Kitchen demo, system design visualization
The Gerontechnology Group, LLC
Dr. Melissa Mansfield, PhD, NAPG-CPG

What this is
A visualization of how P.A.L.S. is designed to read a kitchen. It is not footage of the working prototype.
Thresholds shown are design values, not validated results. The label on the frame says so.

How to view it
Open index.html in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages address for this repository.
The story plays on its own and loops: Mom cooks, moves to the counter with her back to the door,
her dog comes in behind her, the kitchen tells her, she turns and knows he is there.

Recording mode
Add ?record to the end of the address, or press R on the page. Everything but the frame is hidden and
the story loops. Press Esc to leave.

Placeholder
The spoken cue in the frame ("Heads up. Pet behind you.") is placeholder wording, not the final line.
